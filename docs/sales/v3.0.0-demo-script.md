# v3.0.0 Sales Demo Script

> Audience: anyone giving a demo (you, future sales hires).
> Goal: 26-minute walkthrough that lands the value of the platform.
> Format: 6 acts, plus opener + Q&A buffer.

This script complements the [onboarding playbook](../operations/onboarding-playbook.md).
The playbook is what happens AFTER the contract; this script is what happens DURING the sales call.

---

## Setup pre-conditions (before the call)

Run through this list 30 minutes before the demo:

- [ ] Demo environment URL bookmarked + reachable
- [ ] SuperAdmin credentials confirmed working (test login)
- [ ] DEMO tenant present in master.Tenants
- [ ] DEMO tenant has ≥5 products, ≥2 warehouses with locations, ≥3 customers seeded
- [ ] Browser windows / tabs pre-arranged:
  - Tab 1 — SuperAdmin console at `/SuperAdmin/Login` (closed at first)
  - Tab 2 — Tenant login at `/Auth/Login`
  - Tab 3 — Tenant dashboard (signed in, ready to drive)
  - Tab 4 — Mobile PWA on phone or browser device-emulation toolbar
- [ ] Screen-sharing tested (Zoom / Meet / Teams)
- [ ] Backup plan if a critical path breaks (see "Recovery" section)
- [ ] Have a notebook open for capturing customer questions / gaps

## Opener (2 min)

> "Before I screen-share — what does your current WMS hurt at most?
> What would the perfect tool eliminate from your team's day?"

Capture the answer. You'll thread it through the demo: when you hit a
feature that solves their pain, name the connection.

Then:

> "I'm going to walk you through about 25 minutes of the platform.
> Six acts: how a tenant gets onboarded, the first-login experience,
> a full inbound flow, a full outbound flow, operations + reports,
> and security. Stop me anywhere."

Share screen. Open Tab 1 (`/SuperAdmin/Login`).

## Act 1: SuperAdmin Onboarding (3 min)

> "This is the platform console. Behind the customer-facing app.
> I'm the platform operator — I use this to provision new customers."

1. Sign in at `/SuperAdmin/Login` (your SuperAdmin credentials)
2. Land on `/SuperAdmin/Dashboard`
3. Pause on the stat tiles:

   > "Right now I have N active tenants, M suspended. Every event on
   > the platform is logged here — tenant creates, password resets,
   > admin actions. The customer's audit log is separate; this one
   > is for me."

4. Click "Tenants" in the topbar
5. Click "Provision new tenant"
6. Fill the form **live**:
   - Code: `DEMO{timestamp}` (or use a meaningful customer code)
   - Display name: `<Customer's Company Name>`
   - Admin email: `<their primary contact's email>`
   - Admin full name: `<that contact's name>`
7. Click **Provision tenant**

   > "This is creating a brand-new SQL Server database for them,
   > applying ~30 schema migrations, and seeding their first ADMIN
   > user. Takes about 15-30 seconds."

8. Success page appears with the temp password

   > "Here's their temp password. It displays exactly once, here.
   > In a real onboarding I'd send this to them through Signal or
   > a password-manager share, never plain email. They'll be forced
   > to change it on first login."

9. Click "View tenant" → land on TenantDetail

   > "From here I can suspend them (data preserved, login blocked),
   > reactivate, or reset their admin password if they get locked
   > out. Every one of those actions is audited."

**Don't actually suspend — the rest of the demo needs the tenant active.**

## Act 2: First Login + Setup (5 min)

Open Tab 2 (`/Auth/Login`). **DON'T use the freshly-provisioned tenant** — instead, use a pre-seeded DEMO tenant with master data already in place (otherwise the inbound/outbound demos have no products to show).

> "Here's what their admin sees on first login. Same URL their staff
> will use day-to-day."

1. Email: `<DEMO tenant admin>` (e.g. `nwuthipongworachoke@gmail.com` if using bootstrap)
2. Password: `<DEMO password>`
3. Land on tenant selection if multi-tenant (skip if smart-skipped to single tenant)
4. Land on warehouse selection if multi-warehouse (skip if smart-skipped)
5. **If first time** for a freshly-provisioned account, you'd land on `/Account/ChangePassword` here. Mention it:

   > "Bootstrap admins are forced to rotate their temp password. They
   > can't navigate anywhere else until they do. That's why our
   > temp-password-display-once approach works."

6. Land on `/Dashboard`

   > "Real metrics will appear here once they're moving stock.
   > For the demo, focus on the sidebar — every operational module
   > is one click away."

7. Click through the sidebar headings to show breadth:
   - **Inbound** (Purchase Orders / Goods Receipt / Receipts / Receive mobile / Putaway mobile)
   - **Inventory** (Adjustments / Transfers / Locate mobile)
   - **Counts** (Cycle Counts / Cycle Count mobile)
   - **Outbound** (Sales Orders / Pick Tasks / Pack Tasks / Shipments / Pick mobile / Pack mobile)
   - **Master Data** (Warehouses / Products / Customers)
   - **Security** (Users / Roles / Audit Log)
   - **Reports**

   > "Six operational modules + master data + reports + security.
   > Everything you'd expect in a modern WMS. Let me show you a
   > full flow next."

## Act 3: B2B Inbound Flow (5 min)

> "Let's run a purchase order from creation to stock on the floor."

1. **Create the PO** (skip if a draft PO exists you can use):
   - Sidebar → Inbound → Purchase Orders → New
   - Vendor: pick any
   - Warehouse: pick any
   - 2-3 line items with quantities
   - Submit → PO status = Open

2. **Switch to mobile** (Tab 4 on a real phone, or browser device-emulator):

   > "Receivers don't use the desktop. They scan from a handheld."

   - Navigate to `/receive` on the phone
   - Optionally: "Add to Home Screen" prompt visible — show that the PWA installs as an app icon (no Play/App Store needed)
   - Receive queue lists the open PO
   - Tap the PO → task page renders all lines
   - Walk through:
     - Expected vs Received side-by-side per line
     - Quick-adjust buttons (-10 / -1 / +1 / +10)
     - Live variance indicator (✓ Match / ↓ Under / ↑ Over)
     - Location code per line (collapsible fields below)
   - Hit Submit on full-quantity receipt

3. **Show the putaway side**:
   - Navigate to `/putaway` (mobile)
   - Queue shows the just-received stock at the staging zone
   - Tap a row → task page
   - **Suggested location** appears in green:

     > "We tell the operator where to put it. The algorithm clusters
     > picks — if there's already same-product stock at a location,
     > that location ranks higher. Reduces walk time."

   - Tap Submit → stock moves to suggested location

4. **Switch to desktop**, browse Inventory or Reports → Inventory:
   - Confirm the stock shows up at the destination location

   > "Five minutes ago I created a PO. Now it's stock on the shelf,
   > linked back to the PO + the receiver, fully auditable."

## Act 4: B2C Outbound Flow (5 min)

> "Now the other direction — order to shipment."

1. **Create a Sales Order** (or use a pre-created one):
   - Sidebar → Outbound → Sales Orders → New
   - Customer: pick any
   - 1-2 line items (use SKUs that have stock from Act 3)
   - Submit → SO status = Open

2. **Allocate**:
   - On SO Detail, click "Allocate"
   - Walk through the FIFO allocation:

     > "We reserve stock at the moment of allocation. If two orders
     > want the same lot, the first allocator wins. We never
     > double-pick. Today the strategy is FIFO; FEFO is on the
     > roadmap for lot-tracked products."

   - SO status = Allocated

3. **Generate pick task**:
   - "Generate pick" button on SO Detail
   - Lands on PickTask Detail

4. **Mobile picker** (Tab 4):
   - Navigate to `/pick`
   - Queue shows the new pick task
   - Tap → per-line cards
   - Pick at full qty on each line → Submit
   - PickTask status = Picked
   - SO status = Picked

5. **Generate pack task**:
   - On SO Detail, click "Generate pack"
   - PackTask Detail appears

6. **Mobile packer** (Tab 4):
   - Navigate to `/pack`
   - Tap the task
   - Enter Packed qty per line, fill carton metadata (box type, weight)
   - Submit
   - PackTask status = Packed; SO status = Packed

7. **Ship**:
   - On SO Detail, click "Generate shipment"
   - Shipment Detail appears
   - Fill Carrier (e.g. "FLASH") + Tracking number
   - Submit → SO status = Shipped

8. **The wow moment**:

   > "Three minutes ago I created the order. Now it's allocated,
   > picked, packed, sealed in a carton, handed to the carrier
   > with a tracking number. Every step is auditable. Mobile-first
   > because the warehouse staff aren't at a desk."

## Act 5: Operations & Reports (5 min)

> "Day-to-day operations are about visibility. Let me show you the
> reports + the admin surfaces."

1. **Sidebar → Reports → Inventory**:

   > "Total stock across warehouses, aging buckets, top SKUs by
   > quantity, slow movers. All cuts of the same Stock table —
   > we use ApexCharts to render them. Excel export is one click."

   - Click "Export Excel" → real .xlsx downloads
   - Open the file (5 sheets: Summary / By Warehouse / Aging / Top SKUs / Slow Movers)

2. **Sidebar → Reports → Orders**:
   - Show the preset date range chips (Today / 7 days / 30 days / 90 days / Year)
   - Switch presets — charts re-render
   - Donut chart (status distribution), area chart (daily trend), customer + cycle-time charts

3. **Sidebar → Reports → KPIs**:
   - Picks/packs per day line chart
   - On-time shipping % (color-coded green ≥95%, amber ≥85%, red below)
   - Cycle count variance summary
   - Top pickers (operator leaderboard)

4. **Sidebar → Security → Users**:

   > "Admin manages the team from here. Roles are PICKER / PACKER /
   > MANAGER / ADMIN out of the box; custom roles are on the
   > roadmap. Permissions are a function-CRUD matrix per role."

   - Click "New user" → fill the form quickly — point out role checkboxes
   - Or click an existing user → show the role assignment + Quick Actions sidebar (Activate / Deactivate / Unlock / Reset password)

5. **Sidebar → Security → Roles**:
   - Click ADMIN → show the permission matrix grouped by module
   - Note that system roles are read-only

6. **Sidebar → Security → Audit Log**:

   > "Every action with security impact lands here. Filter by user,
   > by event type, by date range. Compliance auditors love this."

   - Show the filter chips + a sample entry's JSON details

## Act 6: Security & Compliance (3 min)

> "Last 3 minutes — security posture. This usually comes up in
> procurement, so let me front-load it."

1. **HTTPS everywhere** (point at the URL bar):

   > "Production deploys behind HTTPS. HSTS header is on,
   > X-Frame-Options DENY, Content-Security-Policy locked down."

2. **Password policy**:

   > "8+ characters, mixed case, ≥1 digit. Failed logins are
   > rate-limited at 5 attempts per IP per minute, plus per-user
   > lockout at 5 failures for 30 minutes."

3. **Audit log** (re-visit briefly):

   > "Everything's logged. Failed logins, password changes,
   > permission edits, tenant suspensions. Immutable — no UPDATE,
   > no DELETE endpoints on the audit table."

4. **Tenant isolation**:

   > "Each customer's data lives in its own SQL Server database.
   > Not a shared DB with a TenantId filter — a fully separate DB.
   > Backup, restore, compliance, all per-tenant. This is the
   > enterprise model."

5. **What's NOT here yet** (be honest):

   > "Two things to mention. We don't have 2FA today — that's
   > v3.1, scheduled within the year. Same for SSO. If those are
   > deal-breakers, let's talk timeline. If not, we move forward."

## Common Questions & Answers (during/after demo)

**"What's your pricing?"**
> See onboarding playbook. Ballpark: $5-15K setup + $15-50K annual
> for v3.0.0 launch tier. Custom — depends on volume + warehouses
> + users. Let's discuss after I understand your scope.

**"Can we integrate with [ERP / e-commerce]?"**
> Not in v3.0.0. We're at "robust core WMS + manual data exchange".
> Integrations are Phase 31+ roadmap. If this is a hard requirement,
> let's talk timeline — we may or may not be the right fit yet.

**"What about [missing feature X]?"**
> Three honest answers depending on X:
> - "On the roadmap, here's the timeline" — show TD-XXX
> - "Not on the roadmap — would you pay for it?" — capture demand
> - "Already there, let me show you" — demo it

**"How long until we're live?"**
> 2-4 weeks typical: 1 week to import master data + train, 1-2
> weeks of supervised use, then go-live. Faster if your data is
> already clean.

**"What happens if you go out of business?"**
> Tenant data lives on YOUR SQL Server (we deploy to your
> infrastructure or a server we manage in your region). You always
> own your data. Standard 30-day data return on contract end. If
> we shut down, you'd still have the database.

**"Show me a customer reference."**
> v3.0.0 is the LAUNCH version — you'd be among the first 1-3
> customers. White-glove onboarding, weekly meetings, your
> feedback shapes v3.1. If reference customers are a procurement
> requirement, we should talk again after the first customer is
> live (3-6 months).

## Wow moments to land

Memorise these — they're the moments that close deals:

1. **Act 1 step 7** — temp password displayed ONCE. Operator
   recognises this is rare in their experience.
2. **Act 3 step 3** — suggested putaway location. Most WMS don't
   tell the operator where to put the stock; they leave it as the
   operator's problem.
3. **Act 4 step 7** — full SO-to-Shipped flow in 3 minutes. Most
   prospects haven't seen all 5 states (Allocated / Picked /
   Packed / Shipped + Cancelled) in one demo.
4. **Act 5 step 1** — Excel export downloads a real, useful file
   instantly. Many WMS export to PDF only.
5. **Act 6 step 4** — separate DB per tenant. Enterprise security
   teams light up at this.

## Recovery if something breaks during the demo

| Failure | Recovery |
|---|---|
| Tenant provisioning hangs > 60s | Acknowledge: "This is normally faster — I'll suspect a network issue. Let me skip ahead and demo on the seeded tenant" |
| Mobile PWA won't load on phone | Use browser device-emulator on the laptop (Chrome DevTools → toggle device toolbar) |
| 500 error on a page | "I'll log this and we'll address it. Let me back up." Continue to the next act |
| Login fails | Use SuperAdmin's reset-admin-password to get a fresh temp password live (demo a recovery!) |
| Database connection lost | "Looks like infrastructure — let me pull up the architecture diagram instead and we'll come back to the live demo when it recovers" |

The customer doesn't expect zero glitches. They DO expect you to recover gracefully. Don't panic; narrate what you're doing.
